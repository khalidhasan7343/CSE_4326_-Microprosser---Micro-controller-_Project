Problem Statement
Manual IV/syringe control causing variability and delays
Limited continuous patient monitoring — higher risk of adverse events
Nurse workload elevated by repetitive, time-critical tasks
Few low-cost, integrated smart solutions for resource-constrained hospitals
Project Objectives
Automatic IV flow control and syringe delivery
Continuous heart-rate monitoring (MAX30102)
Wireless real-time dashboard (ESP32 — ESP-NOW / Wi‑Fi)
Emergency alerts, LCD status, reduced nurse workload

Key Features & Advantages
Dual Infusion
Simultaneous control of two IV lines for parallel therapies.
Automatic IV & Syringe
Precision servo and stepper-based actuation with closed-loop feedback.
Heart Rate Monitoring
Continuous, non-invasive measurement and trend alerts.
ESP32 Wireless
Low-latency ESP‑NOW / Wi‑Fi telemetry to web dashboard.
Advantages
Higher accuracy & consistent dosing
Reduced human error & nurse workload
Continuous patient monitoring and alarms
Low-cost, portable — suited for Bangladesh hospitals
Interface
Local LCD + buzzer for bedside alerts; web dashboard for clinicians.
